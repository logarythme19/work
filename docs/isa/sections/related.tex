\section{Related work and positioning}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Model-based converter control}
State-space averaging supplies the standard low-cost converter model
\citep{Middlebrook1976,Erickson2020}; non-ideal averaged plants with inductor
and capacitor ESR, switch resistance and diode drop have been used to tune
implementable low-order controllers with hardware validation
\citep{Siddhartha2018}, and robust nonlinear or observer-based laws extend
disturbance rejection \citep{Abdelmalek2020,Sitbon2020,Bouchama2020}.
Convex methods---MPC solved as a quadratic program \citep{Liu2018}, LMI regional
pole placement \citep{Chilali1996}---give certificates relative to their model
class but do not make fixed-structure PIDF tuning convex once saturation,
discontinuous conduction and event-wise requirements are included.

\paragraph{LQ design and PID structure}
The LQR return-difference inequality guarantees an infinite upward gain margin,
a gain reduction margin of one half and \SI{60}{\degree} of phase margin for the
full-state loop \citep{Kalman1964,Safonov1977,Anderson1990}; the guarantee is not
inherited by observer-based implementations \citep{Doyle1978}, and loop-transfer
recovery trades it against observer bandwidth \citep{Doyle1979}. Bryson's rule
converts admissible excursions into diagonal weights \citep{Bryson1975}.
Setpoint weighting and back-calculation anti-windup are the standard means of
shaping the command response of a PID and of handling actuator saturation
\citep{Astrom1989,Astrom2006,Bianchi2008}. The connection exploited here---that
the full-state LQI law of a second-order plant with an output-network zero is
a filtered PID of the measured output---is known in nominal transfer-function
form; what is new is its trajectory-level validity and the use of the Riccati
weights, not the gains, as the search space.

\paragraph{Metaheuristic tuning}
PSO \citep{Kennedy1995}, genetic algorithms, ABC \citep{Karaboga2008} and GWO
\citep{Mirjalili2014} with improved variants
\citep{Hou2022,NadimiShahraki2021,Alsamia2024} are widely used to tune converter
controllers \citep{Izci2022,Nanyan2024,ValenciaRivera2024}; surrogate-based
Bayesian optimization is attractive when evaluations are expensive
\citep{Snoek2012,Coutinho2023}. Constraint handling by Deb's feasibility rules
avoids penalty weights \citep{Deb2000}, and matched-seed non-parametric
statistics are the accepted way to compare stochastic solvers
\citep{Demsar2006}. A weighted scalar index does not produce a Pareto front
\citep{RodriguezMolina2020}; we therefore report a constrained scalar problem
and do not claim a multi-objective characterization.

\paragraph{Switching effects in digital control}
In digitally controlled PWM converters, the sampled switching ripple and the
duty resolution can sustain limit cycles that an averaged model does not
predict \citep{Peterchev2003}. This is the mechanism behind the rejection of
averaged-feasible candidates reported in Section~\ref{sec:res:switched}, and the
reason why three inequalities of the present problem are measured on the
switched model.

Table~\ref{tab:gap} positions the study. The gap is not a new metaheuristic but
the conjunction of (i) a structurally meaningful search space whose every point
carries an LQ certificate and an exact output-feedback realization, (ii) a
feasibility set stated in engineering units and partly measured on the
switched plant, (iii) matched equal-budget comparisons that separate the
parameterization from the optimizer, and (iv) a closed-energy-balance switched
validation on the full commanded range.
